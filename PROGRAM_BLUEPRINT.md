# Program Blueprint (Year 1)

## Annual Design

## Cadence

- Monthly in-person executive roundtable (Seattle/Bellevue/Redmond rotation)
- Monthly online session (briefing, peer case review, or workshop)
- Quarterly 3-hour deep-dive forum
- Annual summit (half-day to full-day)

## Suggested Year-1 Themes

Q1:
- Board communication and cyber risk framing
- Building high-trust crisis command across functions

Q2:
- AI governance and model security for enterprise deployment
- Security organization design and operating model maturity

Q3:
- Resilience engineering and business continuity under real incidents
- Third-party and supply chain security decision frameworks

Q4:
- Talent strategy, succession, and leadership burnout prevention
- 12-month outlook and policy/threat horizon planning

## Session Formats

1. Executive Roundtable (In Person, 90 minutes)
- 10-minute host framing
- 45-minute structured peer discussion
- 25-minute tactical takeaways
- 10-minute commitments and next actions

2. Online Working Session (60 minutes)
- 15-minute short expert input
- 35-minute moderated peer exchange
- 10-minute resource and action summary

3. Quarterly Deep Dive (3 hours)
- Scenario simulation
- Breakout strategy work
- Consolidated playbook outputs

## Membership Experience

What members should gain each quarter:
- 1-2 reusable strategic frameworks
- New relationships with relevant peers
- Better executive communication language
- Faster decision-making under uncertainty

## Digital Layer

Use a lightweight digital workspace (private community platform) for:
- Session briefs and reading packets
- Structured post-session notes
- Member directory and expertise map
- Polling for agenda prioritization

## Metrics Dashboard

Track monthly:
- Attendance rate
- Returning attendee percentage
- Session usefulness score (1-5)
- Number of peer introductions completed

Track quarterly:
- Member retention
- Participation spread (avoid over-reliance on few members)
- Sponsor satisfaction (without compromising community rules)
